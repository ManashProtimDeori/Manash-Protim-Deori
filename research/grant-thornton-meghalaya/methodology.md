# Methodology

I used a primary-source-first hierarchy and separated facts, derived facts, synthesis and recommendations. The core thesis was triangulated across 12 institutional sources. Atomic statistics use the canonical source rather than cosmetic repetition of the same number across ten secondary sites. Every fact or insight in the core deck maps to a claim ID; the appendix gives the exact URL, PDF page where applicable, logic/rationale and confidence.

## Important limitations

- The population rural/urban split is Census 2011 based and is not presented as a 2026 headcount.
- The 2021 Youth Policy population estimate is labelled as a policy estimate.
- TRAI subscriptions per 100 population are subscription densities, not unique-user penetration.
- Platform-specific social usage claims were excluded because robust Meghalaya-specific evidence was not established.
- My metrics come only from my CV data.
