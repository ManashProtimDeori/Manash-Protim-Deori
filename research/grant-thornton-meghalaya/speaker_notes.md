# Slide 03 - What I see in the operating context: distance, rurality and administrative spread shape communication design

I would design for the whole state, not let a Shillong-centric content model stand in for Meghalaya.

- **C01 FACT:** Meghalaya had 2,966,889 people in the 2011 Census base used by the state DES portal: 2,371,439 rural and 595,450 urban.
  - Logic: I use this only as a dated structural baseline, not as a current population estimate.
  - S01: https://des.megplanning.gov.in/portal.htm (n/a)
- **C02 DERIVED FACT:** The 2011 DES figures imply that 79.9% of Meghalaya's population was rural.
  - Logic: 2,371,439 / 2,966,889 = 79.93%. This supports designing beyond urban social-media audiences.
  - S01: https://des.megplanning.gov.in/portal.htm (n/a)
- **C03 FACT:** The DES portal lists 12 districts (2022) and 55 C&RD blocks (2022).
  - Logic: Administrative spread makes repeatable district/block communication kits more useful than a Shillong-only content model.
  - S01: https://des.megplanning.gov.in/portal.htm (n/a)
- **C07 FACT:** In the 2024 Independence Day speech, the Chief Minister explicitly linked terrain and distance to difficulty accessing government services and described CM-CONNECT as a response.
  - Logic: The communication architecture should reduce distance through channel redundancy, field touchpoints and clear escalation paths.
  - S04: https://www.meghalaya.gov.in/meghalaya/sites/default/files/press_release/78th_Independence_Day_2024_HCM_Speech.pdf (printed p. 9)

# Slide 04 - I would design for digital reach - without assuming digital uniformity

Digital is essential, but the rural-urban subscription gap and formal field-publicity channels argue for deliberate channel redundancy.

- **C11 FACT:** TRAI reported 2.08 million internet subscriptions in Meghalaya at March 2025, equal to 61.08 subscriptions per 100 population.
  - Logic: Digital channels are material to reach, but subscription density is not the same as unique people reached.
  - S07: https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-07/YIR_08072025_0.pdf (report p. 67 / PDF p. 68)
- **C12 FACT:** TRAI reported 49.45 internet subscriptions per 100 rural population versus 105.53 per 100 urban population in Meghalaya at March 2025.
  - Logic: The gap argues for digital-plus-field distribution; urban figures above 100 also warn against reading subscription density as population penetration.
  - S07: https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-07/YIR_08072025_0.pdf (report p. 67 / PDF p. 68)
- **C14 FACT:** DIPR also describes periodic Special Interactive Programmes across districts and sub-divisions for one-to-one citizen interaction with department officials.
  - Logic: Face-to-face feedback and explanation remain part of the state's communication infrastructure.
  - S08: https://meghalaya.gov.in/index.php/dept/25 (n/a)
- **C17 RECOMMENDATION:** I would use a digital-plus-field channel architecture instead of a digital-only plan.
  - Logic: High rural share, explicit terrain/access barriers, rural-urban internet-density difference, and formal field publicity all point to complementary channels.
  - S01: https://des.megplanning.gov.in/portal.htm (n/a)

# Slide 05 - I would localise meaning, not just translate words

Language matters; so do the example, messenger, context and call-to-action that make a message usable.

- **C05 FACT:** The same RFP specified village-level audience frameworks, communication in local languages and collection of citizen feedback/grievances.
  - Logic: Localisation and feedback are explicit operating requirements in a real Meghalaya citizen-engagement mandate.
  - S02: https://meghalaya.gov.in/sites/default/files/tenders/RFP_for_Selection_of_Agency_for_Grassroot_Citizen_Engagement_Program.pdf (pp. 4 and 23 of 40)
- **C09 FACT:** The Meghalaya State Language Act continues English as the official language and permits Khasi and Garo as associate official languages in specified district-level contexts.
  - Logic: I would treat localisation as audience/context design, with language as one layer rather than a mechanical translation step.
  - S05: https://homepolitical.meghalaya.gov.in/pdf/acts/meghalaya_language_act-2005.pdf (PDF p. 2 / Gazette p. 364)
- **C18 RECOMMENDATION:** I would localise meaning - language, example, call-to-action and messenger - rather than translate one master asset word-for-word.
  - Logic: Local-language requirements coexist with heterogeneous audience needs and culturally appropriate engagement standards.

# Slide 06 - I would design for young audiences without treating youth as one audience
