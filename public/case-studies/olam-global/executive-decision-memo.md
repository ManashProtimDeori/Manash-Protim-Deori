OECD–FAO's 2026–2035 outlook is an external benchmark; extensions beyond 2035 are chosen and bounded. UN WPP2024 medium population paths are external projections, not product demand.

The normalized 25-year project templates use constant 2026 USD, staged capital, commissioning ramps, tax-loss carryforward, capital-cohort depreciation, maintenance, replacement and correctly based working capital. The final year includes 90% NWC recovery and no perpetual franchise value. Managed owned processing has NPV of minus USD1.80 million and peak annual funding need of USD22.56 million. ## Proof, governance and leadership action

Days 1–15 reconcile entity and data. Days 16–30 pre-register the counterfactual, statistical power and cost. Days 31–60 test. Days 61–75 verify cash and transferability. Days 76–90 decide whether to expand, resize, wait or stop. G0–G7 cover perimeter, legal access, customers, contribution, capacity, resilience, cash and replication. Legal and liquidity failures override a demand narrative.

The primary outcome is incremental paid contribution after service, trade and cannibalisation, accompanied by sustained repeat and funding evidence. Sample size must derive from baseline variance, the minimum economic effect, clustering and attrition. No actual Olam treatment effect or pilot budget is asserted.

The executed aggregate food-supply regression offers only a small holdout improvement and is rejected for investment forecasting. Chosen stress quantiles are not calibrated probabilities. The proposed adaptive framework applies established regret, causal measurement and information-value methods; it does not claim a new theorem or a validated optimal global allocation.

Request customer orders, gross-to-net prices, product and route costs, plant constraints, monthly inventory and credit balances, tax and FX records. Appoint Finance, Treasury, Legal, Operations and Marketing owners. Authorize a costed proof programme, challenge its release criteria, and return in 90 days with measured contribution, repeat, cash and a specific next commitment.
