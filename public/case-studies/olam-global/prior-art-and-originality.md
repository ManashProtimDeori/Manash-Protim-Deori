# Prior art and originality assessment

The proposal combines established cash-flow valuation, working-capital identities, constrained optimization, minimax regret, CVaR, real options, Bayesian information value and causal experimentation. Primary references include Rockafellar–Uryasev for CVaR and Callaway–Sant'Anna for difference-in-differences. Aglink-Cosimo provides an institutional partial-equilibrium benchmark. Economic Sciences research context includes Engle/Granger (2003), Romer/Nordhaus (2018), and Card/Angrist/Imbens (2021).

The operating proposal connects customer usable-output economics, physical delivery, seasonal cash, common exposure and learning triggers at country, product, segment, mode, corridor, scenario and horizon level. This may be a useful integration; it is not a new mathematical theorem.

Hypothesis one: transferable commercial learning can outweigh macro market size. Hypothesis two: seasonal working cash can reverse a volume-based ranking. Neither has been validated on Olam internal cases.

Benchmark protocol: hold feasible markets, horizon, maximum capital and information constant. Compare size ranking, stand-alone NPV, expected NPV, regret and adaptive policies. Track out-of-sample regret, cash breaches, returns, decision stability and operating complexity. The executed four-action/six-world example has partner maximum regret of 4 versus owned regret of 8, while owned wins the central case 9 versus 6. Its payoffs are chosen teaching inputs, not empirical results.

Literature coverage is limited to the documented primary sources and methods. It is not an exhaustive patent, academic or commercial-system search. No exclusive originality or empirical superiority claim is warranted.
