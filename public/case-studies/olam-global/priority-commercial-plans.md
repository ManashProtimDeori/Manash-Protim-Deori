## Brazil / Soybeans

Decision unit BRA-soybeans. Threshold-only commercial hypothesis; neither feasible entry nor investment return validated. HS 2022 soybeans other than seed; verify GMO/traceability eligibility; candidate code 120190 is a concordance starting point, not customs clearance.

Customer and need: Crushers, feed processors and import buyers. Protein/oil yield, traceability and shipment continuity. Offer: Qualified supplier lot with crush and quality evidence. Price/pack: Delivered price per usable output; reconcile by-product value. Channel: Approved suppliers, cooperatives and independent offtakers. Promotion/service: Traceability and usable-output proof; no assumed green premium.

Competitive response: Measure delivered rival price, specification, credit and service; allow matched-price/credit response. No shares or rival costs available. Economics: Net paid orders × delivered unit contribution − service/promotion − cannibalisation − cash funding; cap deliveries at verified supply. Test: Incremental paid contribution net of service/cannibalisation; measured usable output and repeat after promotion. Counterfactual: Continue existing service/eligible trade without the incremental intervention, in the same scenario.

Sustainability/access: Verify actual supplier/basin/asset, rights and product standards before any claim or route commitment.
