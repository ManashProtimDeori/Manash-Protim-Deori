# Decision triggers, owners, refresh and model retirement

Version 1.1.0 | Audited 8 October 2026 | Independent public-data research

## Paid repeat / adoption

Owner: Country Marketing. Cadence: Weekly during pilot, monthly after support. Observation/source: Eligible customer payments and cohort repeat. Threshold: Investigate if pre-registered contribution/repeat floor is breached. Action: Reconcile promotion, attrition, spillover and stock loading; stop/resize if economic bound fails. Every alert records timestamp/vintage, economic consequence in native units, uncertainty, investigating owner and closure. Numeric floors and credit/funding limits are set by Finance before pilot launch.

## Price / net contribution / competition

Owner: Finance and Category. Cadence: Weekly lot/route; monthly review. Observation/source: Gross-to-net order, cost and observed rival offer. Threshold: Reprice or wait when contracted margin fails approved floor. Action: Validate quality/credit and competitor response; no macro-price substitution. Every alert records timestamp/vintage, economic consequence in native units, uncertainty, investigating owner and closure. Numeric floors and credit/funding limits are set by Finance before pilot launch.

## Stockouts / service / capacity

Owner: Operations. Cadence: Daily operational review; weekly pilot. Observation/source: Fill rate, uptime, yield, route and stock ledger. Threshold: Marketing capacity headroom≤0 or service breach. Action: Repair bottleneck or cap orders/spend; record contribution lost. Every alert records timestamp/vintage, economic consequence in native units, uncertainty, investigating owner and closure. Numeric floors and credit/funding limits are set by Finance before pilot launch.

## Working capital / collateral

Owner: Treasury and Credit. Cadence: Daily cash/collateral; monthly forecast. Observation/source: AR aging, actual stocks/AP, margin calls, committed cash. Threshold: Projected monthly cash exceeds signed facility/headroom. Action: Pause purchases/spend; collect/reduce stocks; no double-counted release. Every alert records timestamp/vintage, economic consequence in native units, uncertainty, investigating owner and closure. Numeric floors and credit/funding limits are set by Finance before pilot launch.

## Policy / rights / sanctions / HS

Owner: Legal. Cadence: Event driven and before each new commitment. Observation/source: Current gazette/customs/official sanctions and counterparty review. Threshold: Any applicable barrier or unverified clearance. Action: Block action regardless of modelled NPV until verified. Every alert records timestamp/vintage, economic consequence in native units, uncertainty, investigating owner and closure. Numeric floors and credit/funding limits are set by Finance before pilot launch.

## Climate / suppliers / corridors

Owner: Supply Chain and Sustainability. Cadence: Weekly seasonal hazards; event alert. Observation/source: Actual crop/basin/asset/port forecasts and supplier evidence. Threshold: Service/adaptation threshold or rights breach. Action: Qualify distinct backup origin/route; no country-average approval. Every alert records timestamp/vintage, economic consequence in native units, uncertainty, investigating owner and closure. Numeric floors and credit/funding limits are set by Finance before pilot launch.

## Company scope and finance

Owner: Strategy / Finance. Cadence: Quarterly results and ownership event. Observation/source: Correct issuer report, perimeter and contracts. Threshold: Material entity/capability/funding change. Action: Reconcile source conflict and rebuild case version. Every alert records timestamp/vintage, economic consequence in native units, uncertainty, investigating owner and closure. Numeric floors and credit/funding limits are set by Finance before pilot launch.

## Public country drivers

Owner: Strategy data steward. Cadence: Annual WDI; each UN/OECD release. Observation/source: Versioned primary data with missingness and checksum. Threshold: Vintage changes decision or structural regime breaks. Action: Reestimate justified model and compare holdout/decision outcomes. Every alert records timestamp/vintage, economic consequence in native units, uncertainty, investigating owner and closure. Numeric floors and credit/funding limits are set by Finance before pilot launch.

## Model authority and review

Strategy owns versioned model/data; Finance owns cash and editorial reviewer challenges conclusions. Review pilot gates at days15/30/60/75/90 and strategic allocation quarterly/on structural events. Preserve previous versions and realized outcomes. Reestimate only after new independent data and a retained holdout. Retire a model when target mismatch, identification failure, unstable signs, systematic forecast error worse than agreed benchmark, unrepairable missing data or legal/physical assumptions invalidate decisions. The current aggregate supply OLS is already rejected for investment use. No background monitoring automation has been scheduled.
