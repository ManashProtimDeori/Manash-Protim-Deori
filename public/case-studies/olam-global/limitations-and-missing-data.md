## Scope limitations

The 195-country coverage is complete as a disposition/threshold scaffold, not 195 verified investment valuations. Eighteen priority product plans are hypotheses. Population remains WPP Medium only: the compact workbook does not contain low/high variants. Internal/legal/asset/household evidence cannot be manufactured from GDP, national supply or health narratives. No estimated product demand system, gravity, GARCH, crop damage or marketing effect is claimed. The originality search is documented and bounded; actual management-policy comparison, portfolio optimization and external expert review remain open.
