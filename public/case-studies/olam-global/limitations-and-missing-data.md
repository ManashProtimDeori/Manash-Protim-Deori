# Limitations ranked by decision consequence

1. Product/customer orders, capture, net price and costs can reverse contribution and country ranking. All 195 country investment NPVs remain unknown.
2. Monthly seasonal AR, inventory, AP, purchases, tax, FX and hedges can reverse funding and returns. Annual cash omits intra-year peaks; no Olam WACC is asserted.
3. Legal product access, sanctions, rights, usable capacity, yield and route constraints can eliminate a candidate from the feasible set.
4. Competitor prices, shares, service and responses are unavailable at product level. No invented competitive advantage is used.
5. Basin, crop and facility hazards and adaptation cost are missing. Country water stress cannot price asset loss.
6. Diet, health, product consumption and causal adoption remain unmeasured. Public FAO food-balance bulk access failed; no per-person consumption is imputed. No elasticity or Olam marketing effect is estimated.
7. Panel validation uses latest-vintage WDI, complete cases and a weak aggregate supply model. It is rejected for investment forecasting.
8. OECD–FAO's horizon ends in 2035. Extensions through 2051 are chosen scenarios. UN WPP2024 medium population is an external projection.
9. Company sources contain conflicting ownership text and differing tonnage definitions. Dated transactions and defined annual metrics take precedence. Locations are not an asset register or proof of absence elsewhere.
10. Stress and policy examples are chosen, not calibrated probabilities. No optimal all-country allocation or validated mathematical novelty is claimed.

Each country has a next evidence gate. Failed retrievals are logged and not treated as support. No missing financial input is replaced with a fabricated company value.
