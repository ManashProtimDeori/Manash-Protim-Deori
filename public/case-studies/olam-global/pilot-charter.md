# 90-day pilot charter and candidate contribution

Version 1.1.0 | Proposed programme; not an Olam approval

Objective: estimate incremental paid contribution and funding for a bounded Nigeria semolina or edible-oils intervention. Keep category cohorts separate; select a transfer market only after feasibility checks. Finance sets the cost cap, cash breach limit and minimum economically material effect before launch.

## Responsibilities and milestones

Days 1–15: Finance and Country Operations reconcile eligible sell-through, customer IDs, credit, product costs, inventory, route service and capacity. Legal verifies entity and product access. Deliver a signed data dictionary and baseline.

Days 16–30: Marketing and Analytics pre-register the design. Prefer randomized route clusters where feasible. Otherwise use credible matched holdouts with pre-trend diagnostics. Specify outcomes, cannibalisation, spillovers, attrition, treatment timing and inference. Determine sample size from variance, detectable effect and intracluster correlation.

Days 31–60: execute and audit treatment, stock availability, paid orders, usable yield, returns, service cost and stock funding. Separate the intervention from other promotions. Pause or stop according to pre-agreed contribution, legal and cash limits.

Days 61–75: measure paid repeat after support ends; reconcile contribution and cash; run one documented transferability test with local pack, yield, route and credit assumptions.

Days 76–90: Finance independently reviews the effect interval, mechanism evidence and liquidity. Decide expand, localise, resize, wait or stop. Do not extrapolate a short-run effect unchanged through 2051.

## Measures and acceptance

Primary outcome: incremental eligible contribution net of incremental service, trade and cannibalisation. Secondary outcomes: sustained paid repeat, customer usable output, delivery reliability, credit losses, DSO, DIO, DPO and funding. Net ROMI equals incremental contribution after marketing cost divided by marketing cost. Multi-year proposals use full cash-flow NPV.

Actual sample and budget remain unset until baseline data are available. Acceptance requires the approved economic floor, documented cash headroom, no overriding access or rights breach, and understood transfer conditions.
