# Canonical CMO Decision Deck v3 — 10-Iteration Hardening Record

This round extends the existing 20 stakeholder lenses with ten additional iterations focused on marketing decision quality, sensitivity, current 2026 market developments and executive usability.

| Iteration | Test | Gap found | Improvement integrated |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Theme/readability | Global light-theme header rules could make the Canonical page unreadable. | Canonical Strategy Lab is now a self-contained dark decision surface in both site themes. |
| 2 | P&L integrity | Brand and marketing metrics could be mistaken for incremental revenue. | Marketing-influenced ARR is explicitly non-additive; marketing budget is an allocation of modeled reinvestment, not a second cost. |
| 3 | Sensitivity | Static labels did not show which levers materially move economics. | Added local one-step sensitivity for revenue, operating profit, margin and directional brand indices. |
| 4 | Brand linkage | Reach/awareness/strength were not connected to the financial decision tree. | Added a 3D P&L/brand bridge with rebased brand indices (100 = default scenario). |
| 5 | Competitive response | The previous deck did not answer “what does this change do to competitors?” for every input. | Every active variable now shows Canonical impact, competitor impact and the marketing response. |
| 6 | Security/regulation | 2026 CVE acceleration and CRA changes were missing. | Added rapid kernel SRU cadence, Ubuntu 26.04 security and Zephyr/CRA implications. |
| 7 | AI/agentic | AI story risked being generic infrastructure positioning. | Added secure agents, Open Secure AI Alliance, local agentic PC, governed data and silicon-readiness narratives. |
| 8 | Partner leverage | Partner value was mainly an ARR input. | Added partner amplification as a separate marketing/distribution variable with transfer and falsifier logic. |
| 9 | Human decision behavior | Technical B2B buying psychology was implicit. | Added message clarity, community trust, analyst authority, switching-risk and champion-to-executive retellability. |
| 10 | Executive usability | Senior leaders needed a “what do I do now?” output. | Added always-on directional insight boxes, marketing rescue actions, falsifiers and latest-development radar. |

## Guardrails

- Reported financial facts remain source-backed.
- Scenario assumptions remain visible and editable.
- Brand indices are directional decision diagnostics, not audited brand-equity measurements.
- Marketing-influenced ARR is not added to revenue.
- Customer TCO and sustainability outputs require customer-specific evidence before external use.
- Competitive implications are strategic inferences, not claims about competitor intent.
