# Canonical Executive Strategy System — 20-Pass Review Record

This file documents the twenty challenge lenses applied before the live deck was promoted for deployment. The purpose is not to claim certainty. It is to make the strategy falsifiable and to prevent hidden assumptions from surviving behind presentation polish.

| Pass | Stakeholder / lens | Hard question | Change integrated into the deck/model |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Investor | Can recurring growth accelerate without destroying operating leverage? | Added explicit contribution margins, reinvestment and P10/P50/P90 ranges. |
| 2 | Investor | Is growth quality improving, or are services masking software weakness? | Subscription and services are modeled separately; subscription mix is a guardrail. |
| 3 | Board | Which bets deserve disproportionate capital? | Reduced the corporate narrative to assurance, private-cloud migration and neutral AI infrastructure. |
| 4 | Finance | What is the break-even reinvestment rate? | Added a calculated reinvestment ceiling from incremental contribution economics. |
| 5 | Finance | How fragile is the plan to retention deterioration? | Paid-base retention is a first-class assumption and Monte Carlo driver. |
| 6 | CIO | Why migrate rather than renew the incumbent stack? | Added editable customer TCO, payback and three-year savings. |
| 7 | CISO | Is lifecycle still differentiating? | Corrected Ubuntu to up to 15 years and acknowledged RHEL 14+ years; repositioned around broader assurance. |
| 8 | Developer/community | Could monetization damage the adoption engine? | Explicit rule: monetize assurance/accountability, not core freedom/adoption. |
| 9 | Cloud partner | Does Canonical complement or disintermediate hyperscalers? | Reframed Canonical as portable workload assurance and infrastructure substrate. |
| 10 | OEM/SI partner | Is there enough economic value for channel partners? | Partner-sourced ARR is explicit and measured at workload creation/expansion. |
| 11 | Product | Is the platform real, or just bundleware? | Every component must map to one business outcome and an expansion path. |
| 12 | AI leader | Why Canonical when clouds already sell AI stacks? | Focused the wedge on neutral OS/Kubernetes/private-sovereign infrastructure and hardware readiness. |

| 14 | Sovereign-cloud buyer | Can the architecture work under sovereignty constraints? | Elevated portable private/hybrid infrastructure and local control. |
| 15 | Sustainability | Can impact be quantified without greenwashing? | Added instrumented kWh/workload and carbon-intensity inputs; removed universal savings claims. |
| 16 | Sales | Which accounts should be prioritized? | Added trigger-based prioritization: regulated, VMware-heavy, AI, high-Ubuntu footprint. |

| 20 | Model governance | How will leadership know the model is wrong? | Added primary-source register, explicit assumptions, deterministic tests, uncertainty bands and falsifiers. |

## Model governance rules

1. **Reported fact** must trace to a dated source.
2. **Assumption** must remain visible and editable.
3. **Strategic inference** must have a leading indicator or falsifier.
4. **Scenario output** must never be described as Canonical guidance.
5. **Customer economics** must be replaced with customer-specific data before external commercial use.
6. **Sustainability outputs** require measured energy baselines and location-specific carbon intensity before external claims.
7. Every production build runs the Canonical strategy arithmetic/sensitivity test.
8. The public site is a non-destructive sandbox. Owner drafts are local until promoted through GitHub.
